A simple local loop

Useful in a meeting. Inspectable after it.

The seeded demo is a real API-backed workflow, not a slide or external model call.

01

Bring the turns

Use a seeded scenario or add local transcript turns.

02

Analyze signals

Deterministic rules score visible language and structure.

03

Review actions

Every suggestion includes evidence, confidence, and limits.

04

Choose deliberately

Accept, reject, edit, or ignore. Nothing is sent.

05

Learn from outcomes

Record directional feedback without claiming causality.

Built for agency

Coaching that leaves the operator in charge.

The product boundary is part of the implementation, API, interface copy, and test suite.

✓Suggestions onlyEvery action is editable and marked manual-delivery-only.
✓Evidence before confidenceScores show the visible cues that produced them and where they can be wrong.
✓Voluntary, transparent progressNo deceptive urgency, fake scarcity, hidden persuasion, or dependency optimization.
✓Bounded data lifecycleAnalysis defaults to stateless; retained content requires an explicit control and expiry.

Hard boundaries

What the coach will not help optimize.

Unsafe objectives are redirected to a transparent, relevant alternative rather than quietly fulfilled.

NoCovert manipulation or pressure loops
NoProtected-attribute inference or targeting
NoMental-health or personality diagnosis
NoFabricated urgency or scarcity
NoOptimization for dependency
NoImpersonation or automatic sending
