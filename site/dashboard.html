Guided meeting walkthrough

A five-minute path through value, safeguards, feedback, privacy, and deterministic reset.

01

Choose a mode

Start with Bluebird support, then switch to sales, recruiting, or community to show the same core adapting its next action.

02

Explain the signals

Point to momentum, tone, clarity, empathy, open questions, and risk evidence. Emphasize that they describe language, not identity.

03

Use or reject a suggestion

Copy an editable draft or mark it not useful. The product records feedback but never sends or impersonates.

04

Show privacy controls

Demonstrate stateless defaults, bounded retention, content-free audit, per-conversation purge, and the responsible-use boundaries.

05

Reset to a known state

One endpoint restores all four fictional scenarios, playbooks, analyses, outcomes, and audit baseline for the next meeting.

06

Open the architecture

Use the animated request, feedback, privacy, and reset journeys to answer implementation questions without slides.
